You are the city manager. The city is in crisis and you have twelve quarterly decisions to bring it back. Nothing here tells you a number about the city's state. No population, treasury, satisfaction or health score. Worn roads look worn, unpowered buildings go dark, derelict lots look derelict. That is deliberate: the agent modes cannot see those numbers either, and the comparison is only fair if you are reading the same city they are. To get numbers, ask the data.
